City Tourist

Privacy

What the City Tourist app and this website collect, where it goes, and how to have it deleted.

In short: the app has no ads and no analytics, and you don't need an account to use it. Your trips stay on your iPhone. To plan them, the app asks a few services for places, events, weather and routes. If you use the AI features, what you type is sent to Anthropic's Claude. If you choose to sign in, your phone number or your Apple sign-in is kept so you can sign in again. This website keeps the email you sign up with and any survey answers you send, and nothing is sold to anyone.

The app

Your trips stay on your device

Trips, stops, saved places and settings are kept on your iPhone. We don't receive a copy. Deleting the app deletes them.

Location, for Today and directions

If you allow it, the app uses your location on the Today screen to work out how long it takes to reach your next stop and when to leave, and in Directions to draw a route from where you are. Those routes are worked out by Apple Maps. You can type a starting point instead of sharing your location. Your location isn't sent to us or stored by the app. You can turn location off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Services the app asks, and what they get

  • Google Places: the city and area you're browsing, what you search for, and the places on your trips, so it can show places, opening hours and photos. Covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • YouTube: the name and city of a place you open, to find short videos of it. A video you tap plays in YouTube's own player, so YouTube sees what you watch. The app uses YouTube API Services, and by watching videos in it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The list of videos found for a place is kept on your iPhone for up to a week. Covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • Ticketmaster: your trip's city and dates, to find events on during it. Covered by Ticketmaster's privacy policy.
  • Eventbrite: the city's location and the dates, to find more events. The request goes through our own server at Supabase, which passes it on and doesn't keep your internet address.
  • Open-Meteo: the city's location and your trip's dates, for the forecast and sunset times.
  • Apple Maps: the stops (and, on Today and in Directions, your location or the starting point you type) between which it times or draws a trip, and addresses you type, such as where you're staying. Covered by Apple's privacy policy.

If you tap More on TikTok or Instagram on a place, their app or website opens with the place's name. What happens there is covered by their own privacy policies.

Like any app that goes online, these requests come from your device's internet address. They don't include your name, your email or your phone number.

AI suggestions and trip plans

The AI concierge and "Plan my whole trip" are answered by Claude, a model made by Anthropic. When you use either, the app sends Anthropic what you typed or chose, the city, your trip's dates when a whole trip is being planned, and public facts about a list of places there, such as names, areas, ratings and opening days. It doesn't send your location, your saved places, your other trips, your name or your phone number. So don't type anything private into the request. Covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.

The plan that comes back is checked on your iPhone against opening hours and travel times. Nothing from the AI features is used unless you tap to add it to a trip.

Signing in

Signing in is optional, and the app works the same without it. You can sign in with your phone number or with Apple. With a phone number, you are texted a code to confirm it; the number and your sign-in are kept by Supabase, our database provider, and the text is sent by the messaging service it uses. With Apple, Apple confirms who you are and passes Supabase an identifier for your account and an email address, which is a private relay address if you choose to hide yours. If Apple shares your name, it's used to fill in your profile and stays on your iPhone. The app keeps you signed in with a token stored in your iPhone's keychain.

If you add a profile photo while signed in, it's stored with Supabase where only your account can read it. The name on your profile stays on your iPhone. Your trips aren't uploaded when you sign in.

Reminders

Leave-by reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They aren't sent from a server.

Shared trip links

When you share a trip, the plan is written into the link itself. It isn't stored on our servers. Anyone you send the link to can see the plan.

TestFlight

If you test the beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports, basic usage such as installs and sessions, and any feedback you send through TestFlight with us. Covered by Apple's privacy policy.

This website

Beta sign-up

When you sign up, we keep your email address and, if you gave it, your first name. We use them only to email you about the City Tourist beta. They're stored with Supabase, our database provider. A note that your address has signed up is also kept with Cloudflare, so you aren't emailed twice.

The beta survey

Survey answers are stored with Supabase, along with the city and app version the app adds to the link. They don't include your name. Your email is included only if you choose to leave it for a follow-up question.

Hosting and limits

The site runs on Cloudflare. To stop someone flooding the forms, your internet address is kept for up to an hour, with a count of how many times the forms were sent from it. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your internet address the way any website you visit does.

Your choices

  • To have your sign-up or survey answers deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, email tmwakalasya@icloud.com.
  • To stop beta emails, reply and say so, and we'll take you off the list.
  • To sign out, use Sign out in the app's Settings. To have your sign-in, phone number and profile photo deleted, email the address above from any account and tell us the phone number, or the email address shown under Account in the app if you signed in with Apple.
  • To remove everything the app keeps on your iPhone, delete the app.

Children

City Tourist isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date. If the change matters, we'll tell beta testers by email first.
